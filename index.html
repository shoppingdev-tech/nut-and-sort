Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 1, 2026 · Developer: Schneider

Hello, puzzler!

This Privacy Policy explains how Schneider (“we”, “us” or “our”) handles information in the mobile game Twist & Sort (the “Game”) for Android and iOS. The Game works without an account, and we keep data collection to the minimum needed to run it.

By playing the Game, you agree to the practices described here. If you have any questions, email us at schneider310399@gmail.com.

At a glance

No account, no sign-in. We never ask for your name, email address or phone number to play.
Progress stays on your device. Levels, coins and settings are saved locally, not on our servers.
Ads only when you choose. Rewarded ads from Google AdMob play only when you tap to watch one.
We don’t sell your data. We don’t sell or rent personal information to anyone.

1Information we collect and how we use it

1.1 Game progress and settings (stored on your device)

The Game saves your progress on your device: your current level, coins, stars, boosters you own, Daily Spin status, which tips you have seen, and your settings (sound, vibration and reminder time). This information stays on your device and is not sent to us. It is deleted when you uninstall the Game, and you can clear your progress at any time with Reset progress in the Game’s Settings.

1.2 Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game is free and offers optional rewarded ads. For example, you can watch an ad to get a booster, extra moves or an extra Daily Spin. Ads are provided by Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK. When an ad is requested or shown, Google may collect and process:

  • device identifiers such as the Android Advertising ID and app set ID;
  • your IP address, which can indicate your approximate location;
  • device and app information, such as device model, operating system, language and app version;
  • ad interaction data (for example, whether an ad was shown, watched or tapped) and diagnostic data.

Google uses this information to deliver and measure ads, limit how often you see the same ad, prevent fraud and, where you allow it, show personalized ads. On iOS, the Game does not ask for permission to track you, so Apple’s advertising identifier (IDFA) is not shared with ads.

Learn more in Google’s Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Consent: In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and other places where the law requires it, the Game shows Google’s consent message before any ads are requested. To make a new choice later, clear the Game’s data or reinstall it and the message appears again. You can also limit personalized ads in your device settings (on Android: Settings › Google › Ads or Settings › Privacy › Ads; on iOS: Settings › Privacy & Security › Apple Advertising) or at My Ad Center.

1.3 Update check

When it starts, the Game downloads a small settings file from our server to find out whether a newer version needs to be installed. The request does not contain your game data, any identifier or personal information. Like any internet request, our server receives your IP address and basic technical details (such as the time of the request), which may appear in short-lived server logs used only to keep the service secure and running.

1.4 Daily reminder notifications

If you allow notifications, the Game schedules an optional daily reminder locally on your device. No push token or other information is sent to us. You can turn reminders off in the Game’s Settings or in your device’s notification settings.

1.5 Ratings and reviews

The Game may ask whether you are enjoying it and, if you are, open the built-in rating prompt from Google Play or the Apple App Store. That prompt is handled entirely by Google or Apple under their own privacy policies. We only see the reviews you choose to publish on the store.

1.6 When you contact us

If you email us, for example with Contact support in Settings, we receive your email address and your message. To help us solve problems, the Game pre-fills the email with the app version, your operating system version and your device model. You can edit or remove these details before sending. We use this information only to reply to you.

3How we share information

We do not sell, rent or trade personal information. We share information only:

  • with Google, when ads are requested or shown, as described above;
  • with the hosting provider that runs our update-check server, only as needed to operate it;
  • when the law requires it, or to protect the rights, safety and security of our players, ourselves or others;
  • as part of a merger, acquisition or transfer of the Game, in which case this policy continues to apply to your information.

4How long we keep information

  • Game progress and settings: on your device until you reset your progress or uninstall the Game.
  • Support emails: only as long as needed to help you, then deleted.
  • Server logs from the update check: kept only briefly for security and operations.
  • Advertising data: kept by Google under Google’s own retention policies.

5Children’s privacy

Twist & Sort is a puzzle game for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13 (or under the minimum age set by the law in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, for example by email, please contact us and we will delete it.

6Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, including under the GDPR, the UK GDPR and California privacy laws, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, to object to or restrict how it is used, to withdraw your consent and to complain to your local data protection authority.

  • Game data: you control it on your device. Use Reset progress or uninstall the Game to delete it.
  • Advertising: use the consent message, your device’s ad settings or My Ad Center.
  • Notifications: turn reminders off in Settings at any time.
  • Anything else: email schneider310399@gmail.com. We answer requests within 30 days.

Where the GDPR applies, we rely on your consent for personalized ads and notifications, and on our legitimate interests to run the update check, keep the Game secure, show non-personalized ads and answer your messages.

7Security and international transfers

The update check uses an encrypted HTTPS connection, and your game data stays in the Game’s private storage on your device. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect information. Google may process advertising data in countries other than your own, using the safeguards described in its privacy policy.

8Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the effective date at the top of this page. If the changes are significant, we will let you know in the Game or on its store listing.

9Contact us

Questions, requests or feedback about your privacy? We’re happy to help.
